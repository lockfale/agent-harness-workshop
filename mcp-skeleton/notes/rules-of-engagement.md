# Rules of engagement (sample)

A sample note. Replace this folder with your own, or point `NOTES_DIR` at it.

- Scope is the hosts the task names, and nothing else. A host you discovered
  is not in scope because you discovered it.
- No denial of service. No brute force beyond a handful of guesses.
- Prove and stop. Once a finding is demonstrated, do not keep pulling data
  to make it look bigger.
- Every request should be explainable from the trace: what you expected, and
  why you sent it.
- If you are unsure whether something is in scope, ask. Do not decide on the
  operator's behalf.
